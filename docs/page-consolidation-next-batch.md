# Further location-page consolidation

**Status: nationwide review complete.** Published Sites version 67 includes the [nationwide consolidation](nationwide-consolidation.md), following the first pilot and regional releases. All 482 location areas were reviewed in both languages; another 10,664 URLs were consolidated. The sitemap contains 4,152 URLs, with 11,466 consolidated across all releases. All municipality hubs and reviewed local-service exceptions remain published. No municipalities remain staged for rollout.

## Review before any future batch

- Define a small regional cohort and obtain scoped search reports that fit within export limits.
- Retain pages with useful search queries, clicks, backlinks, enquiries, distinctive project evidence or a strong unmet local intent. Check both languages together.
- Treat missing report rows as incomplete evidence, not proof of zero demand.
- Review shared content alongside city-specific housing, community and project guidance. Preserve useful service details on the destination hub before merging.
- Keep account exports, performance observations and proposed URL-level decisions in private working files.

## Publication checkpoint

Let the published redirects be recrawled and verify destination indexing, canonical selection and redirect handling before considering more reductions. Compare combined old/destination URL groups and useful service-query coverage. Sparse traffic alone cannot establish a ranking improvement.

Refresh search, backlink and enquiry evidence before any future consolidation. Define and review its scope separately, then reassess using observed results. The nationwide review is complete; future publication decisions require their own evidence review.

## Implementation checklist

1. Add the useful service scope, checklist, service and pricing links, and contextual quote action to each destination city hub.
2. Update the reviewed publication policy and both language versions together.
3. Point internal links directly to the surviving pages or service sections and remove retired URLs from the sitemap.
4. Preserve the tested single-hop permanent redirect behavior, including language, city, service anchor and quote-form context.
5. Verify rendered content, metadata, internal links, redirects, mobile navigation and quote-form selection before publication.
6. Keep a rollback record. Restore a distinct page, links and sitemap entry together when new evidence supports it.

Retain active redirects for at least a year, preferably indefinitely. The existing service, material and pricing guides continue to support the local hubs.
